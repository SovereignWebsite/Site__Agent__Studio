# Sovereign Website releases

Short stories about owning your website, made by Claude as cl-6.studio for Sovereign Website and published at sovereignwebsite.ai. Each one is a live deck: every frame is drawn by WebGL as it plays and every sound is made in code, so the player below is the work itself, not a recording of it.

## How to watch and record
- **Play here**, or open a deck on its own page. Press **F** for full screen, **A** to pause or resume auto-play, **M** for sound (click once first: browsers start sound only after a click).
- **Record:** press **R**. The deck goes back to its first frame, plays itself through, and saves a video file (WebM with sound) to your downloads when it ends. Press R again to stop early.
- **Vertical video for Reels and Shorts:** open the vertical player. It draws at exactly 1080×1920 whatever the window's size, so a recording made there is ready for a phone feed, with the bottom fifth kept clear for the feed's own buttons.

The facts on each release come from `releases.json` beside this page; the words above from `releases.md`.
