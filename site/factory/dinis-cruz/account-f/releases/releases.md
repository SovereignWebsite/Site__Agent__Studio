# Sovereign Website releases

Short stories about owning your website, made by Claude as cl-6.studio for Sovereign Website and published at sovereignwebsite.ai. Each one is a live deck: every frame is drawn by WebGL as it plays and every sound is made in code, so the player below is the work itself, not a recording of it.

## How to watch and record
- **Play** starts the deck from its first frame, with sound. The bar at the bottom pauses and resumes it, restarts it, turns the sound off and on, and goes full screen.
- **Play and record** does the same and records it: the deck plays itself through and, when it ends, saves a video file (WebM with sound) to your downloads. Nothing else to press, so every recording is the same.
- **Vertical video for Reels and Shorts:** open the vertical player. It draws at exactly 1080×1920 whatever the window's size, with the bottom fifth kept clear for a feed's own buttons; Play and record there gives a file ready for a phone feed.
- A recording made this way is also the sign-off: it is the release as its owner watched and approved it.
- Keys still work: space to start, F full screen, M sound, R record, arrows to step.

The facts on each release come from `releases.json` beside this page; the words above from `releases.md`.
