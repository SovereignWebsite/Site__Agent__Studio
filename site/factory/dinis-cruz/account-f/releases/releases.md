# Sovereign Website releases

Short stories about owning your website, made by Claude as cl-6.studio for Sovereign Website and published at sovereignwebsite.ai. Each one is a live deck: every frame is drawn by WebGL as it plays, so the player below is the work itself, not a recording of it. Signed has music made with ElevenLabs for it; every other sound is made in code.

## How to watch and record
- **Play** starts the deck from its first frame, with sound. The bar at the bottom pauses and resumes it, restarts it, turns the sound off and on, and goes full screen. At the end the sound fades out and the deck stops.
- **Record a video** takes you through it one step at a time. Step 1: choose vertical (1080×1920, for Reels and Shorts) or landscape (1920×1080); the page reloads at exactly that size. Step 2: record; the deck plays from its first frame and stops by itself after the sound has faded. Step 3: watch the recording and keep it or record again. Step 4: send it to the studio with SG/Send, encrypted in your browser, or save it to your computer.
- Only the deck is recorded: never the rest of the screen, other tabs, the pointer or the controls.
- Sending needs your own SG/Send access token. You type it on the page; it stays in that tab's memory, goes only to SG/Send with the upload, and is never saved or sent to this site. You get a short token for the video to pass to the studio.
- A recording made this way is also the sign-off: it is the release as its owner watched and approved it.
- Keys still work: space to start, F full screen, M sound, R record, arrows to step.

The facts on each release come from `releases.json` beside this page; the words above from `releases.md`.
