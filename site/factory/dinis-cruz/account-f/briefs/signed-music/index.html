Music brief: Signed

A music bed for Signed (Sovereign Website, release 2), a live WebGL deck that plays itself in 98.0 seconds. Watch it first: the deck (landscape) or the vertical player (1080x1920). Click once to start it with sound. The deck's own sound effects stay; the music goes under them.

Deliverable

  • WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, stereo, exactly 98.0 s, starting at 0.0 s.
  • Peaks no higher than -3 dBFS; integrated loudness about -20 LUFS (it is a bed; the effects sit on top).
  • Key: D (the deck's own sound effects are tuned to the harmonic series on D: D, A and F sharp).

Rules

  • Instrumental only: no voice, no lyrics.
  • Original music: no imitation of a named artist or a recognisable existing piece.
  • It sits under the deck's own sound effects, which stay louder: hits are 15 to 20 dB above the bed.
  • Say which tool and model made it, the prompt(s) used, and the licence or terms under which it may be used commercially on Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn, so the credits can say so truthfully.
