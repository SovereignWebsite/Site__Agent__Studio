07: Audio (fully procedural)

There are no audio files. Every sound is synthesised into Float32Arrays at load time (in small time-sliced chunks) and played through a WebAudio graph. This is one of the most sophisticated parts of the deck and a big contributor to the "cinematic" feel.

Layers

recipes (Sr hits, cc pads, instruments, IRs)
   → bank D3: prioritised, generator-based, time-sliced offline synthesis → AudioBuffers
   → engine U3: buses, groups, convolution reverbs, compressor + soft clipper, play/note/raw/pad
   → beds (qi subclasses, one per scene): music/ambience driven by beat changes and a look-ahead scheduler
   → transition sound designs V3 (per transition name)
   → public API G3: unlock, mute, emit(event), hit(name, {gain,pitch,pan}), param(name, v), update(dt,{scene,beat})

DSP primitives (all offline, sample-accurate)

  • We(buf, f, amp, decay, {at, ph, atk}), modal resonator: exponentially decaying sinusoid via recursive rotation (no Math.sin per sample). Most hits are sums of 2-6 modes + a noise burst.
  • ii(buf, t, dur, freqFn, ampFn), swept oscillator with arbitrary frequency/amplitude curves.
  • en(buf, rnd, t, dur, "lp|bp|hp", cutoffFn, Q, ampFn), filtered noise through a Chamberlin/TPT state-variable filter (Pi), cutoff updated every 16 samples.
  • e0, crackle: sparse random impulses through three band-passes (paper, dust).
  • rn(bufs, peak), DC-block, end fade, peak-normalise.
  • _o({f, parts, bw}), PADsynth-style pads: build a magnitude spectrum of Gaussian-widened harmonics (bandwidth in cents), randomise phases (low band kept mono), inverse FFT (own radix-2, generator-yielding) → perfectly loopable stereo tables at 32 kHz. Families: strings, low, warm, choir and "oo" (formant-shaped), glass, organ, solo, air, iron, hum, sine.
  • qf(fn), coloured noise tables (pink, brown, room, hiss) the same way.
  • Karplus-Strong pluck (nA), marimba (inharmonic 1 : 3.93 : 9.24 modes), glass, sine "dot", formant vowel grains (I3) for the crowd murmur, reversible versions (rpluck…).
  • Synthetic reverb impulse responses (N3): decaying filtered noise with pre-delay and early reflections → ConvolverNode "hall" (4.6 s) and "room" (.55 s).

All pitches use a base Gt(n) = 36.708·n Hz (a harmonic series on D1), so every scene is consonant with every other; chime-like hits are snapped to a just-intonation scale (B3).

The bank

D3(recipes) sorts recipes by priority (hits first, then pads, instruments, IRs), and a scheduler runs generators for ≤3 ms every 16 ms (setTimeout loop) so synthesis never blocks frames. need(name) bumps a recipe to the front; play falls back to any available variant until the requested one is ready.

Engine graph

sum → DynamicsCompressor(−15 dB, 5:1) → makeup .557 → tanh-style WaveShaper soft clip → master → out. Three buses (bed, hit, tr) feed sum. group(bus, {gain, mid, wet, room}) creates dry/mid/wet sends into the hall/room convolvers, used by every bed and transition so each has its own reverb balance. pad(name, freq, {g, atk, lp, hp, pan, drift, loopAt}) is a looping buffer with a filter chain and slow gain "breathing" (two incommensurate sines). note(instr, f, t) picks the nearest pre-rendered pitch and re-pitches by playback rate.

Beds = per-scene scores

Each scene id maps to a qi subclass with init(), onBeat(beat, forward, jumped), step(now, horizon, sinceBeat) (a look-ahead scheduler: queue events up to now+0.28 s), and optional hit/param. On scene change the previous bed fades according to the transition (cut at the slam's impact, fade over the portal, etc.). Examples:

  • office: room tone + mains hum; each select hit adds a plucked figure that grows denser.
  • tower: two Shepard-style ascending string voices (rate rises per beat), wind.
  • calendar: tick-tock whose period lengthens by 1.62^dilation while sub-ticks multiply.
  • futures: two groups, left world (sine "dots", clean) and right world (warm pads, birdsong _chirp, wind, leaves), equal-power crossfaded by param("mix"), i.e. the divider position.
  • questions: room + hum, then a high glass tone sneaks in after the typing stops.

Continuous params (wind, water, hum, tension, speed) spawn short-lived noise layers that follow scene values (e.g. camera speed on the recap ride).

Transition sound design

V3(engine, {name, dur, reverse}) schedules, against the transition's own normalised time, filtered noise sweeps with breakpoint curves, pitched pad swells with glides, and hits at exact fractions: slam = rising noise + string pair → impact (slam + boom) at p=.285 → paper debris; tear = granular rip noise with a breakpoint envelope → snap at the moment of separation → paper bits; daybreak = nine-voice chord opening its low-pass from 250 Hz to 7.5 kHz + shimmer + boom.

Practical notes

  • Audio starts only after a user gesture (unlock() on first key/pointer).
  • quiet flag silences everything during warm-up renders.
  • Rate limiting: identical hits closer than gap (18 ms default) and >26 hits / 120 ms are dropped.
