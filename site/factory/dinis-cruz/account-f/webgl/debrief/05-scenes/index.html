Narrative device worth copying

The deck starts and ends on a deliberately ugly default-template slide (Arial, grey page number, blue default chart series, a blue footer bar via Wf). Everything in between is spectacle. The contrast is the point, and the recurring SelectBox marquee ties them together.

Deep dive, futures ("Two aims")

Beats: (1) "Same power. / Different aim." captions on each side; the divider drifts on its own (sinusoid around the centre) and is draggable. (2) The divider slides right, the "living" world takes over, "The forecasters are asking for a pause. / AI Futures Project, 10 November 2026". (3) Divider goes to 0 (living world full screen), light opens up, "It is not decided yet."

Two complete worlds, one terrain. uWorld uniform = 1 (living) / 0 (optimised). Both share:

  • Noise textures generated at init: a 256² RGBA (fbm, finer fbm, Worley, hash) rendered on GPU, and a 64³ two-channel Data3DTexture built on CPU (tiled 3D Worley + value noise) for clouds.
  • Height function Sh(x,z) (CPU, also sampled for placing objects): domain-warped fbm; ridges as Gaussian "capsules" along hand-placed polylines ([x,z,height,width] control points); smooth-max blending; a mountain range only beyond z < −60; a carved riverbank; a raised plateau for the hero tree. En(x,z) adds terraces (quantise height to 0.42 steps with a smooth riser) in garden masks.
  • Camera-fitted terrain mesh: 480 rows × 570 columns where row distance grows exponentially from the camera (d = 4·(235/4)^(v/480)) and width grows with distance, triangles are roughly constant in screen space. Normals by central differences. A 200×240 low-res copy is used for the reflection pass. Per-vertex aM: distance to footpath, garden mask, forest density, terrace mask.
  • Baked lighting: orthographic top-down height render (1536²) → pass that marches 110 steps toward the sun (horizon shadow) and 40 golden-angle AO samples → texture tBF (far, 224×216 units) and tBN (near, 26×18 units) sampled through baked(xz) and shadow(w, bias).

Living world contents (all instanced): procedural recursive trees (branching tubes with jitter, leaf cards) in 2 species; ~4,300 placed trees; 84,000 grass blades that bend with a gust field and carry flowers; hundreds of houses (a jittered 62×36 candidate grid filtered by terrace mask, slope and noise, plus clusters around villages) on terraced slopes (3 roof types, window grid shader, chimneys); three bridges (arched and suspension, generated from a parametric deck curve); four waterfalls (noise-scrolled ribbons + spray sprites); a hero tree on a plateau with per-leaf sway; boats with sails orbiting ellipses and heeling in gusts; two kites with strings; seven gulls; three bird flocks (74 birds with flapping); 170 drifting petals; 64 people walking a Catmull-Rom path or milling about, bobbing.

Optimised world contents: the same terrain covered by white slabs placed with an adaptive quadtree (subdivide where slope, noise or screen size demand), heliostat-like tilting tops with travelling "lift" waves, stone conveyors moving in lanes, a sea filled by tiles rising row by row, collectors on stalks tracking the sun, crate trains, square-lattice "swarm" in the sky occulting the sun, a glass-box pavilion around the hero tree, the hero tree bleached grey with one white leaf, glass-flat water.

Water (l2): grid in camera space; normals from three scrolling noise octaves with gust-scaled amplitude and depth-scaled damping; Fresnel mix of baked-shadowed water colour (shallow→deep by baked depth) and the mirror-camera reflection; sharp + broad sun specular; shoreline foam bands sin(depth·3.4 − t) + whitecaps in gusts.

Sky + clouds (Fb, r2): sky gradient + sun disc; volumetric clouds raymarched at 0.42 res in a 15-54 unit slab, coverage = 13 drifting hand-placed blobs × noise, density = 3D texture eroded by two finer octaves, 5-step light march, two-lobe extinction, ambient by height, distance haze; temporal reprojection (jittered start, golden-ratio frame offset, previous VP matrix, blend 0.13, 16 warm-up frames on reset). The optimised sky instead draws three layers of moving rectangles ("perfect lattices overhead, all moving at exactly one speed").

The split: divider T ∈ [0,1]. Each frame: clouds only for the right part; J(world, …, x0, x1) renders reflection then main pass for each world scissored to its side into a shared 4× MSAA HDR target; one gfx.post; captions rendered scissored per side; divider drawn by its own shader (line + three discs, hot state on hover). pointer() grabs within 60 units of the line; on release the divider eases back to the choreographed path. Audio params wind and hum follow T.

Deep dive, pile ("You are here")

  • The curve y = e^(x/6) from x=−170 to 37, arc-length parameterised with a 6000-sample cumulative table and binary search (V0(s) → {x,y,tx,ty}).
  • A ribbon of "paper" along the whole curve; each slide is a 16×9 strip at arc position Hr(i) = s0 + i·(9+1.15), textured with ctx.snapshots[id] (live captures taken as the presenter left each scene) or thumb-<id>.jpg; empty dashed frames before and after.
  • Camera starts framing the last slide (portal geometry matches the previous scene's frame), rides down the curve with ioExpo and a sinusoidal pull-back, lands beside "today", then beat 2 tilts up at the wall of slides with a wider FOV: "This is the flat part."
  • 260 instanced sheets drift down; the date is new Date(); the SelectBox frames today's slide via rectOf. Audio param speed follows camera speed.

Deep dive, questions ("Any questions?")

Beat 1: white slide, Arial "Any questions?", footer bar, grey page number. Beat 2: a blinking caret, "I have one." types in (with typing sounds) and a SelectBox pops around it. Beat 3: the text deletes, "What do you want?" types, the SelectBox grows to the whole screen, then the entire slide is rendered to a target and redrawn through a pass that rotates it by ~1° (with drop shadow on a dark background) and the marquee rotates with it, with a creak sound.

Deep dive, office (opener) building blocks

  • Canvas2D chart (X3) redrawn into a CanvasTexture only when its parameters change (cache key).
  • A list of "panels", each {color, update(t), render(out), rect()}, background rect + overlay + optional extra pass; rect() returns where the word sits so the SelectBox can track it across styles.
  • Raymarched SDF word: map(p) extrudes the 2D SDF texture with an animated height, soft shadows, cheap AO, orbiting camera.
