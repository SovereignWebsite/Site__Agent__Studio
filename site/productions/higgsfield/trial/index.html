What Higgsfield is for here

Supporting, not replacing: “Higgs field is a supporting, not a replacement.” (Dinis Cruz, 28 November 2026)

It does

  • Key-frame stills as internal storyboard references
  • Plates and textures used as a layer inside a WebGL scene
  • Poster frames, one per production

It does not

  • Replace a WebGL scene with a clip
  • Any person, face or crowd of human figures; never a named author
  • Any words inside a generated frame
  • Fake archival footage or documents

Recorded for every generation

  • test
  • model or mode
  • prompt
  • references
  • seed if any
  • credits or cost as the account shows it
  • wall time
  • usable yes or no, and why (judged by higgsfield.c against the rubric, then c.productions; Dinis decides on anything shown in a scene)

Failures are recorded as well as keepers. Files go into a vault, never this repository; anything shown carries “Image made by AI” inside the frame.
