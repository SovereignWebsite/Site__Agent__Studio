# Agile Cells: script, v0.1

1. **hook** (6 s): To deliver one task, we built a small company. Then we closed it.
2. **values** (17 s): In 2001, seventeen people wrote down four values. Individuals and interactions, over processes and tools. Working software, over comprehensive documentation. Customer collaboration, over contract negotiation. Responding to change, over following a plan.
3. **question** (6 s): Agile Cells asks: can Agile scale, and go back to those values? And to focus?
4. **cell** (11 s): An agile cell is a small team of people and agents, formed for a piece of work and ended after it. Its roles, its rules and its work are written down as files anyone can read.
5. **scale** (9 s): A cell can have ten roles, or twenty, thirty, fifty. Almost a mini company, with its own org chart, just to deliver one task.
6. **proof** (14 s): We did this for a film. One person, two maestros on two accounts, a conductor, a lead agent, scene builders, a critic, test viewers and a designer. Set up for one film, and archived when it is done.
7. **verticals** (10 s): And a cell can be what you ask for. A social media cell. A website cell. A CRM cell. A design cell. An agent behaviour policy cell.
8. **end** (4 s): Agile Cells. A small company, for one task.
