# Agile Cells: script, v0.5

1. **critique** (11 s): Martin Fowler, one of the Manifesto's authors, in 2018: the Agile Industrial Complex imposing methods upon people, and that to me is an absolute travesty.
2. **intro** (8 s): Agile Cells: the early values, at scale. Here is how a cell works, and here is one, working for real.
3. **values** (9 s): In 2001, seventeen people wrote four values. The first: Individuals and interactions over processes and tools.
4. **cell** (11 s): An agile cell is a small team of people and agents, formed for one piece of work and ended after it. Its roles, rules and work are files anyone can read.
5. **scale** (9 s): A cell can have ten roles, or twenty, thirty, fifty. Almost a mini company, with its own org chart, for one task.
6. **made** (10 s): And here is Agile working. One cell made a real film, How we refresh a session, version after version, in one day.
7. **proof** (8 s): One person, a lead agent, scene builders, a critic and test viewers. Set up for that one film, and taken down when it is done.
8. **close** (10 s): So Agile can scale: a cell for each task, built when it is needed, closed when it is done, its files kept. That is Agile Cells.
