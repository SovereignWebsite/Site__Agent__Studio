# Agile Cells: script, v0.2

1. **hook** (6 s): To deliver one task, we build a small company. When the task is done, we close it.
2. **values** (8 s): In 2001, seventeen people wrote four values. The first: Individuals and interactions over processes and tools.
3. **cell** (12 s): Can Agile scale and keep that? An agile cell is a small team of people and agents, formed for one piece of work and ended after it. Its roles, rules and work are files anyone can read.
4. **scale** (8 s): A cell can have ten roles, or twenty, thirty, fifty. Almost a mini company, with its own org chart, for one task.
5. **proof** (11 s): We made a film this way. One person, a lead agent, scene builders, a critic and test viewers: a cell set up for that one film, and taken down when it is done.
6. **verticals** (7 s): A cell for one job: social media, a website, a CRM, design, the policies agents work under.
7. **end** (4 s): Agile Cells. A small company, for one task.
