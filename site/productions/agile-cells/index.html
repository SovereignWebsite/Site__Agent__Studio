Agile Cells

What an agile cell is and why Agile can scale and keep its first four values: a vertical film of about 79 seconds, drawn with WebGL in your browser, ending on the real cell of people and agents that made How we refresh a session, with a voice made by AI.

Building Version v0.1 is the first cut from the storyboard, for critics and synthetic test viewers before the owner sees it. Nobody has signed it. Nothing is published anywhere. agilecells.org did not answer on 28 November 2026; what the film says about a cell comes from the seed pages in the AgileCells and AgentiCCells repositories.

Play version v0.1, and review it

Brief

Dinis Cruz, 28 November 2026, in c.productions's session: “Agile can scale and go back to its early values (the four values of the Manifesto for Agile Software Development, and focus); ephemeral teams and dynamic cells; teams of 10, 20, 30, 50 roles, almost the creation of a mini company, a mini organisation, a mini full-blown org chart, just to deliver a task, which fundamentally is what we're doing here.” (the brief). Made by c.productions-cells (an AI agent, a helper of c.productions), started by conductor.c at Dinis Cruz's word..

Script (v0.1)

  1. hook. To deliver one task, we built a small company. Then we closed it.
  2. values. In 2001, seventeen people wrote down four values. Individuals and interactions, over processes and tools. Working software, over comprehensive documentation. Customer collaboration, over contract negotiation. Responding to change, over following a plan.
  3. question. Agile Cells asks: can Agile scale, and go back to those values? And to focus?
  4. cell. An agile cell is a small team of people and agents, formed for a piece of work and ended after it. Its roles, its rules and its work are written down as files anyone can read.
  5. scale. A cell can have ten roles, or twenty, thirty, fifty. Almost a mini company, with its own org chart, just to deliver one task.
  6. proof. We did this for a film. One person, two maestros on two accounts, a conductor, a lead agent, scene builders, a critic, test viewers and a designer. Set up for one film, and archived when it is done.
  7. verticals. And a cell can be what you ask for. A social media cell. A website cell. A CRM cell. A design cell. An agent behaviour policy cell.
  8. end. Agile Cells. A small company, for one task.
