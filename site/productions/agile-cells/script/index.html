Agile Cells: the script

The film as a screenplay: what is seen, what is said and what is written on screen, scene by scene. Version v0.3, 8 scenes, 60 seconds, 160 words of voice.

Voice: Mistral voxtral-mini-tts-3, made by AI and labelled on screen in every frame. The picture directions are the storyboard’s.

1. A COMPANY FOR ONE TASK 0:00 to 0:05, scene component cells-grow

Points of light snap into an org chart in a second or two, thirty or more roles, then fold down onto a shelf as a stack of files.

Fast build, a held beat on the full chart, a quick fold. A preview of beats four and six.

VOICE

To deliver one task, we build a small company. When the task is done, we close it.

ON SCREEN

A chart of 50 roles folds onto a shelf of files.

2. FOUR VALUES 0:05 to 0:14, scene component snowbird-page

The one page of the Manifesto, its four lines lit in turn: the left half bright, 'over' in amber, the right half dim.

Reused: lab/snowbird-page, span 9.6 to 14 s, as in The story of Agile. The four values quoted exactly from agilemanifesto.org.

VOICE

In 2001, seventeen people wrote four values. The first: Individuals and interactions over processes and tools.

ON SCREEN

Individuals and interactions over processes and tools; Working software over comprehensive documentation; Customer collaboration over contract negotiation; Responding to change over following a plan

3. WHAT A CELL IS 0:14 to 0:25, scene component cells-grow

Inside the membrane: one warm light (a person) and a few cooler ones (agents). Three cards slide out of the cell, labelled roles, rules, work.

The cell forms, the members join one by one, the files fan out beside it.

VOICE

Can Agile scale and keep that? An agile cell is a small team of people and agents, formed for one piece of work and ended after it. Its roles, rules and work are files anyone can read.

ON SCREEN

roles, rules, work

4. TEN ROLES, OR FIFTY 0:25 to 0:34, scene component cells-grow

The cell grows in steps, 10, 20, 30, 50, a counter beside it; inside, the lights arrange themselves into an org chart with branches.

Each step lands on a word of the voice. Ends on the full chart (the same frame the hook previewed).

VOICE

A cell can have ten roles, or twenty, thirty, fifty. Almost a mini company, with its own org chart, for one task.

ON SCREEN

10, 20, 30, 50 roles

5. WHAT A CELL MADE 0:34 to 0:39, scene component refresh-trailer

VOICE

We made a film this way: How we refresh a session, seven versions in one day.

ON SCREEN

How we refresh a session, as live cards of its scenes.

6. WE DID IT 0:39 to 0:48, scene component refresh-cell

The real chart of the cell that made How we refresh a session, growing from Dinis Cruz down to the helpers, drawn round as a cell, then archived to a shelf with its files.

Reused: lab/refresh-cell, span 0 to 14 s.

VOICE

The cell: one person, a lead agent, scene builders, a critic and test viewers. Set up for that one film, and taken down when it is done.

ON SCREEN

Dinis Cruz; account C: c.maestro, conductor.c, c.productions with 5 scene-smiths, a critic, 5 test viewers and 2 helper sessions, and design.c, higgsfield.c, accountant.c; account B: b.maestro, higgsfield.b, elevenlabs.b. An agile cell. Archived: its files stay.

7. A CELL FOR ONE JOB 0:48 to 0:56, scene component cells-verticals

Five cells stacked down the tall frame, each a membrane with its own lights and a short label, one lighting up per word.

Each cell buds off the one above, in time with the voice.

VOICE

A cell for one job: social media, a website, a CRM, design, the policies agents work under.

ON SCREEN

social media, website, CRM, design, agent behaviour policy

8. AGILE CELLS 0:56 to 1:00, scene component cells-verticals

The title over the five cells, dimmed. No web address until agilecells.org answers. The AI voice label shows here only.

A still title, held.

VOICE

Agile Cells. A small company, for one task.

ON SCREEN

Agile Cells. Five cells, dimmed.
