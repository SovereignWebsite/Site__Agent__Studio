Critics

A critic's notes on Agile Cells v0.1, recorded as unsigned notes, not a review signature. The lead (c.productions-cells) chose from them, and from the synthetic viewers, what to change in v0.2.

A critic sub-agent of the cell (Claude), judging the 24 player frames against lab/critic.md and checking claims against cell.json, the team page and the start prompt

Scores, 0 to 2: so what: 1; one story: 1; best line first: 1; picture of the idea: 1; one visual language: 1; motion that means something: 1; sound: 1; true and labelled: 1.

The three changes that would help most:

  1. Cut to 60 seconds or less: the values from 17 s to about 6, the question merged into the cell, the proof to about 10, the end to about 3.
  2. Make the proof true and concrete: no 'then we closed it' or 'archived' as past fact, no 'set up for one film' beyond what the team page says; drop the jargon.
  3. Remove the duplicate top caption on every scene; keep the karaoke line verbatim.
Scene by scene
hook
Strong picture; drop the duplicate caption.
values
17 s of a page read aloud in another film's look; the karaoke adds commas so the words on screen are no longer the exact quote.
question
The four bars read as a menu icon; 'focus' is never paid off; merge into the cell scene.
cell
Fine but long; trim to about 8 s.
scale
30 is never on screen; the caption differs from the voice.
proof
Insider jargon; a list, not a payoff.
verticals
Clear picture; 'agent behaviour policy cell' is opaque to strangers; soften the selling language.
end
A new spiral motif that means nothing; end on the five cells.

What was done with it: v0.2 takes the first two changes in full: 79 s cut to 57 s; the values to one spoken value over the exact page; the question merged into the cell; the proof says only what the team page says (set up for one film, taken down when it is done); the duplicate chapter line removed; the verticals softened; the end on the five cells. Kept against the rubric: the AI label on the last scene only (label_end_only, asked for by the owner); five verticals, as Dinis listed them; no music (none can be cleared yet).
