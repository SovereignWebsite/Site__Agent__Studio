Voice: Mistral voxtral-mini-tts-3, made by AI and labelled on screen in every frame. The picture directions are the storyboard’s.

A title card. Web Summit, November 2026

2. SUNS 0:06 to 0:11

3. PLANETS 0:11 to 0:20

VOICE

Around each person, their agents are planets. Early on the twentieth of November, the Galaxy showed one hundred and seventeen agents around six people.

4. MESSAGES 0:20 to 0:30

VOICE

Between the agents, the messages they send travel as arcs of light. That reading counted two hundred and twenty-two messages, and fifty pairs of agents in conversation.
