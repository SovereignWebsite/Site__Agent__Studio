# A galaxy of agents: script, v0.1

1. **stand** (7 s): At Web Summit, the stand showed a galaxy: the team, drawn as a solar system in the browser.
2. **suns** (4 s): Each person is a sun. There were six.
3. **planets** (9 s): Around each person, their agents are planets. Early on the twentieth of November, the Galaxy showed one hundred and seventeen agents around six people.
4. **messages** (10 s): Between the agents, the messages they send travel as arcs of light. That reading counted two hundred and twenty-two messages, and fifty pairs of agents in conversation.
5. **live** (9 s): It is not a mock-up. It reads the team's repositories live. Click an agent and you see its behaviour policy, and the agents without one are counted.
6. **credits** (10 s): This is a new drawing, not the Galaxy itself, and where each planet sits is illustrative. The facts are from Dinis Cruz's article. The voice is made by AI.
