How we refresh a session: the team that made it

One person, one lead agent, and a small cell of sub-agents and outside models set up for this one film and taken down when it is done.

Models as recorded on 28 November 2026. The lead's model is read from its own session record. Sub-agents started by the lead take the lead's model unless another is set, and none was set; that was not checked run by run. Outside models are named as the service reported them.

How the work moved

  1. The owner gives a brief and a document of facts.
  2. The lead writes a storyboard: every beat with a sketch, its words and its numbers.
  3. Five scene-smiths build the five scenes at the same time and judge their own frames.
  4. The lead looks at every frame, joins the scenes with a voice, and publishes the version for review.
  5. Critics and synthetic viewers judge it; the lead chooses what to change.
  6. Each scene-smith does one more round; a new version is cut.
  7. The owner watches and speaks his feedback; the loop runs again.
  8. When the film is signed, the cell is taken down. Its files stay.

Every run, with its minutes and tool uses, is in cell.json and drawn on the productions page. Data for this page: team.json.
