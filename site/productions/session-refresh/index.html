How we refresh a session

How a long AI agent session is handed over to a fresh one, and what it saves: a vertical film of about a minute and a half, ten scenes drawn with WebGL in your browser, every number from one measured session and one measured hand-over, with a voice made by AI.

Script (v0.1)

  1. hook. This agent wrote three quarters of a million tokens. To do it, it re-read ninety-five million.
  2. why. Every turn, a chat re-reads itself from the top. So each message costs a little more than the last. That is a straight line. Add the lines up, and you get a curve.
  3. moment. The rule says: hand over before three hundred thousand tokens. Every hand-over we can evidence came later. The latest, at eight hundred and forty-five thousand.
  4. writes. So the old session writes down what the next one needs. What is open. What it learnt. What to do first. A few pages, in the repository. Nothing may live only in the session.
  5. reads. A person starts a new session with one line. It reads the rules, its role, its state. It never opens the old transcript.
  6. keys. Some things are lost on purpose. The old key is retired; the new session cuts its own. Secrets sealed to the old key are sealed again, and checked to open.
  7. test. The test: does the fresh session reach the same next step as the old one? If not, a file is missing. Fix the file.
  8. saving. One measured hand-over. Before: eight hundred and sixty thousand tokens of context. After: one hundred and nineteen thousand. Eighty-six per cent less to re-read, on every turn. About seventy-five thousand is a floor that stays.
  9. lesson. Write it early. Keep state in files. Load only what you need.
  10. credits. Every number here has a source, listed below the player. There is no presenter, and the voice you hear is made by AI.
