Whose website is it? With the cast

A draft for Dinis's review, before the full video is cut. It is a quick preview: 10 frames a second, at half size. The final cut runs at 30 frames a second in full 1080 by 1920.

The ten beats

  1. The usual website: Mocho dozes off, Rowan points at the question. Whose website is it? For most people, someone else's: on their server, with their template, under their terms.
  2. A site you own: everyone looks up at the clouds. A sovereign website is one its owner holds: the files, the history and the address.
  3. Rented or owned: The Caretaker walks the divider as its handle slides across. Same web, two ways to be on it. Rented: their server, their template. Owned: your files, your history, your address.
  4. Static files: Cubo rolls over the tiles, which light up. Every page is a static file. Eight thousand one hundred boxes here, drawn in one go.
  5. No server code, cookies or tracking: a plug is pulled, a cookie flicked away, a tracking eye chased off. No server code to run, no cookies to accept, and no tracking. Out they go.
  6. Every step kept: KEPT stamps on the filmstrip. Every step so far is kept, as it was when we left it. The history is yours too.
  7. Move it anywhere: a site frame carried through the ring. It can move anywhere, at any time. It is plain HTML.
  8. Real screenshots: one of our own sites, its files, its history, its address. And this is real. One of our own sites: its files, every change kept, and its own address.
  9. How it was done: from cl-6.studio's README. How was it made? Seven scenes of live WebGL in one HTML file, with no image, video or audio files. Every sound is made in code.
  10. End card: everyone bows; sovereignwebsite.ai and .org. Want a website you own? Find us at sovereign website dot A I.

How it is made

  • The film: cl-6.studio's deck "Whose website is it?" in its portrait layout, filmed with its own film.cjs (113 s, 30 frames a second, its sound made in code). The deck's own words and notes are the source for beats 1 to 7.
  • The cast: The Guide, The Caretaker, Cubo, Mocho and Rowan from the PrompTugal presenters library (bulletins.b, CC BY 4.0), unchanged, rendered offline and keyed over the film. This page never loads the library (b.security's ruling of 26 Nov 2026).
  • The voice: Mistral Voxtral, voice James (voice made by AI), 25% faster; the characters take turns.
  • How it was done (beat 9): only what cl-6.studio's README says: seven scenes of live WebGL in one HTML file, no image, video or audio files, every sound made in code.
  • Screenshots (beat 8): public pages only: the Instagram platform site's files and commit history on GitHub, and the live site.
  • Covers: with the cast and for the native version.
